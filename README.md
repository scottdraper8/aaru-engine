# Aaru Engine

Aaru Engine is being built to help you choose Genshin Impact teams from the
characters and equipment you actually own, and understand what changes when you
move an item between characters.

**Not ready for real account recommendations yet.** The first milestone is a
private, local-first website with realistic sample inventories. Sample data and
illustrative mechanics will always be labelled; they are not verified game facts.

## What to expect

- Open the website in a modern browser on Windows or Linux.
- Try a sample account before sharing any of your own information.
- Choose what matters to you: damage, comfortable play, or a balanced team.
- See the assumptions and trade-offs behind a suggestion, not just a score.
- Compare item changes, including what the previous owner loses.

The calculation engine is written in Rust. The website will run it on your own
computer. No Genshin password, game automation, paid AI service, or desktop
installer is part of this design.

An account export may contain private information. Never post your export in an
issue or send it to someone you do not trust. Battle Chronicle alone does not
include every unequipped weapon or artifact, so it cannot provide a full inventory.
