# Aaru Engine — player-facing identity

The owner requires Genshin-themed user-facing features and tools, grounded in an
understanding of the game's terminology and quests. Keep **Aaru Engine** as the
product name. The supplied naming discussion is design context; independently
verify exact quest quotations and lore claims before publishing them as facts.
Do not turn unverified plot summaries into canonical game/mechanics data.

## Direction

Sumeru scholarship, Aaru/Deshret's engineered knowledge systems, and the Akasha
provide the identity: structured knowledge, relationships, alternate possibilities,
and understandable evidence. Favor warm sandstone, restrained gold, deep teal or
lapis accents and geometric motifs, with accessible contrast and reduced-motion
support. Use original styling, system/local fonts, and no unlicensed game assets
or remotely loaded artwork/scripts/fonts.

Potential labels (use only when the corresponding feature actually exists):

These are product labels, not assertions that the game uses all these names for
these exact functions. Satyavada Life is a state/interface concept, not a machine
or place; do not redefine it in explanatory lore. Use **Elemental Resonance**
only for the actual supported game mechanic, not as a false claim that every
arbitrary synergy score activates a resonance buff.

## Clarity and trust

- Pair unfamiliar themed names with plain-English descriptions. Do not require
  lore knowledge to use the website. No elaborate role-play in instructions.
- Keep important actions literal: **Import account file**, **Export account**,
  **Try a sample account**, **Clear local data**, **Compare weapon move**.
- Never disguise file import as account login, a game connection, or a privileged
  Akasha/HoYoverse integration. The account remains on the user's computer.
- Errors explain what happened and how to recover. Do not substitute jokes or
  “Forbidden Knowledge” for a useful validation message.
- Synthetic accounts and illustrative mechanics remain clearly labelled in the
  interface. The theme must not lend false authority to unsupported facts.
- Avoid story spoilers by default. Any optional lore explanation needs a source,
  accurate terminology, and a spoiler warning where appropriate.
- Use in-game terms correctly: characters, weapons, artifacts, refinement,
  constellation, talents, party, rotation, energy recharge and elemental aura.
  Explain jargon where it first matters to a novice.
- No renamed developer systems or empty feature pages solely for flavor. Apply
  thematic vocabulary to genuine user-facing interactions and keep it consistent.
